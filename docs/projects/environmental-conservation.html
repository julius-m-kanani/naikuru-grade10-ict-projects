Overview

Problem: Few trees, dusty/eroded patches, no tree records, low ownership of green spaces.

Question bank tip: pick 10–12 Qs from ≥4 themes, must include 1 ICT Q (Theme H). Example themes: tree cover, soil/erosion, water, attitudes, actions, effects, solutions, ICT (database of trees, WhatsApp reminders, photo journal, spreadsheets).

Task 1 — Collect & Analyse

  1. 12-Q field set: Role, TreesNearClass? (0/1–3/4+), GreenRating 1–5, ErosionSeen?, WateringWho?, TopCause (no planting/no watering/goats/no records), Effect?, PlantedBefore?, WillingToPlant?, TopSolution?, ICTUseful?
  2. Headings: RespID, Role, TreesCount, GreenRating, ErosionSeen, WateringWho, TopCause, Effect, PlantedBefore, WillingToPlant, TopSolution — starter: environment-starter.csv
  3. Formulas: =AVERAGE(D2:D21) green rating • =COUNTIF(E2:E21,"Yes") erosion • =COUNTIF(J2:J21,"Yes")/COUNTA(A2:A21)*100 % willing • =COUNTIF(G2:G21,"*water*") • =IF(D2<=2,"Priority zone","OK")
  4. Charts: bar of TopCause; pie of WillingToPlant; map/list of priority zones.

Task 2 — Report

Causes: no planting plan, no watering rota, goats, no records. Effects + photo of eroded/dusty spot vs green spot. Solutions table: 20 seedlings + map | watering rota | tree database (ID/species/planter) | digital noticeboard progress | petition to county. Include tree-record table as appendix.
