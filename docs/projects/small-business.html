Overview

Problem: Kiosks/canteen lose money: no daily records, unknown profit, stock-outs, cash mixing.

Task 1 — Collect & Analyse

  1. Survey 20: 5 sellers + 15 customers. Qs: Item, BuyingPrice, SellingPrice, QtyPerDay, KeepsRecords?, TopChallenge (no book/no pricing/stock-out/debt), RecordsHelpful?
  2. Two sheets: (A) Survey + (B) 7-day sales log: Date, Item, Qty, UnitPrice, Total(=Qty*Price), Cost, Profit(=Total-Cost) — starter: business-starter.csv
  3. Formulas: =B2*C2 total • =D2-E2 profit • =SUM(D2:D8) week sales • =SUM(F2:F8) week profit • =AVERAGE(...) • =MAX/MIN best/worst item • =COUNTIF(...,"No") no records • =IF(F2<0,"Loss","OK")
  4. Charts: bar of profit per item; line of 7-day sales.

Task 2 — Report

Causes: no record book, guess pricing, no stock count, credit/debt. Effects + photo (stock shelf, permission). Solutions table: daily sales book template | pricing formula (price = cost + margin) | weekly stock count | debt list | simple receipt in Word. Include your 7-day profit table. Mail merge price list to 10 classmates.

Task 3 — Slides

Title → Week sales line chart → Best/worst items → Record-book demo → Pricing rule → "Every shilling recorded".
