Fillable: open fillable questionnaire.

Task 1 — Collect & Analyse (13)

  1. Short 12-Q field version: Class, StudyHrs, HomeworkHrs, SleepHrs, PhoneHrs, HasTimetable?, MeetsDeadlines?, Postpones?, Rushes?, TopDistractor?, Effect?, TopSolution?
  2. Headings:
    RespID, Class, StudyHrs, HomeworkHrs, SleepHrs, PhoneHrs, HasTimetable, MeetsDeadlines, Postpones, Rushes, TopDistractor, Effect, TopSolution
    Code answers as numbers where possible (e.g. StudyHrs: <1=0.5, 1–2=1.5, 2–3=2.5, 3–4=3.5, >4=5). Starter: time-management-starter.csv
  3. Formulas (rows 2–21):
    =AVERAGE(C2:C21) avg study • =AVERAGE(F2:F21) avg phone • =MAX(C2:C21)/=MIN(C2:C21) • =COUNTIF(G2:G21,"Never") no timetable • =COUNTIF(I2:I21,"Always")+COUNTIF(I2:I21,"Often") chronic postponers • =COUNTIF(K2:K21,"*phone*") phone as top distractor • =IF(F2>C2,"Phone > Study","Study ≥ Phone") flag • =CORREL? (skip) — instead compare averages: phone-heavy vs study-heavy groups.
  4. Charts: bar of avg hours (Study vs Homework vs Sleep vs Phone vs Leisure); pie of MeetsDeadlines; bar of TopDistractor. One is enough, two is excellent.

Task 2 — Report (21)

  1. Description: avg study vs phone hours, % with no timetable, % postponing.
  2. Causes bullets: phone/social, no timetable, procrastination, household duties, fatigue.
  3. Effects + photo (study desk / prep room, no faces) — late submission, stress, poor performance.
  4. Solutions table: personal timetable template | to-do + alarms | study groups | phone-free prep | counselling/study-skills.
  5. Attach a 1-week sample timetable as appendix/figure. Mail merge it to 10 classmates.

6 slides: Title → Hours chart → Habits (timetable/deadlines) → Distractors → Solutions (sample timetable image) → Pledge ("My 2-hour rule").
