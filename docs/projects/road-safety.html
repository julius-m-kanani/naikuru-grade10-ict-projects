Overview

Problem: Learners cross busy roads to/from school — boda-boda overload, no zebra crossing, phone distraction, speeding near gate.

Your questionnaire (real, from your class): Sections A–I: General info → Travel → Awareness (Q9–13) → Behaviour (Q14–18) → Safety near school (Q19–23) → Effects (Q24–25) → Solutions (Q26–29) → ICT (Q30–33) → Opinions (Q34–37). Full printable fillable version: open fillable questionnaire.

Field tip: Use the 12-question short version for 20 respondents (5 min each), keep the full 37 for deep dives.

Task 1 — Collect & Analyse (13)

  1. Short questionnaire (pick these 12): Role, Distance, TravelMode, RoadsCrossed, TaughtSafety?, KnowsZebra?, CrossBehaviour?, Helmet?, PhoneWhileWalking?, CrossingNearGate?, WitnessedAccident?, TopSolution?
  2. Survey 20+. Suggested spreadsheet headings:
    RespID, Role, Distance, TravelMode, RoadsCrossed, TaughtSafety, KnowsZebra, CrossBehaviour, Helmet, PhoneUse, CrossingNearGate, WitnessedAccident, TopSolution
    Starter file: road-safety-starter.csv
  3. Formulas to use (rows 2–21):
    =COUNTA(A2:A21) n • =COUNTIF(D2:D21,"Walking") walkers • =COUNTIF(D2:D21,"Boda*") boda users • =COUNTIF(H2:H21,"*phone*") risky crossing • =COUNTIF(J2:J21,"Never") phone-always • =COUNTIF(L2:L21,"Yes*") witnessed • =COUNTIF(M2:M21,"*Speed bump*") • =COUNTIF(D2:D21,"Walking")/COUNTA(A2:A21)*100 % walking • =IF(J2="Always","High risk",IF(J2="Often","Medium","OK"))
  4. Charts (at least 1): bar of TravelMode; pie of PhoneWhileWalking (Always/Often/Sometimes/Rarely/Never); bar of TopSolution. Title + labels required.
  5. Save GroupXX_Task1_Spreadsheet.xlsx. Full click-by-click: analysis guide.

Task 2 — Report (21)

  1. Title + intro (Naikuru centre road, who you surveyed).
  2. Description: % walking/boda, roads crossed, % never taught safety, % phone use.
  3. Causes (bullets): no zebra, faded signs, speeding, boda overload/no helmet, phone distraction.
  4. Effects + 1 photo (gate road at dismissal, faces blurred) + witnessed-accident stat.
  5. Solutions table: speed bumps | painted zebra | prefect patrol + posters | assembly campaign + petition letter (mail-merge this letter!) | boda rules.
  6. Format + auto TOC + mail merge to 10 classmates. Builder: fillable report builder.

Task 3 — Slides (14)

6 slides: Title → How learners travel (chart) → Riskiest behaviours (phone/boda stat) → Gate safety rating → Solutions → Call to action ("Look both ways. Prefect patrol Fridays."). Design + transitions + 1 animation/slide max.
