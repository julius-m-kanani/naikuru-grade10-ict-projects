TASK 3: PROJECT PRESENTATION

STEP 3: FINDINGS SLIDE WITH CHART (SLIDE 3)

This is the most-marked slide: it must show a chart or graph reused from Task 1 plus a one-sentence takeaway. Never type raw data tables — show the picture.

Example: Waste findings (n=20)

Key Findings

What our survey found (n=20)

Plastic 10 (50%)
Food 6 (30%)
Compound 4 (20%)

Takeaway: 6 in 10 learners do NOT use bins (Bin 40%, Pit 25%, Ground 20%).

Source: Task 1 spreadsheet
3 / 6

Steps: copy chart from Excel correctly

  1. In Excel/Calc: right-click chart → Copy.
  2. In PowerPoint: Home → Paste → Paste as Picture (so it never breaks on another computer).
  3. Add Chart Title + data labels in Excel BEFORE copying.
  4. Below chart type one takeaway sentence (examiner reads this).
  5. Animations → chart → Wipe. Nothing else on this slide.

Chart per topic

  • Waste: bar of waste types. Road: bar of travel mode + pie of phone use. Time: bar of avg hours. Business: line of 7-day sales.
