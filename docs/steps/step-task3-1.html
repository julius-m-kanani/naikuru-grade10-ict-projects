TASK 3: PROJECT PRESENTATION

STEP 1: CREATING THE TITLE SLIDE

In this step, you will learn how to create the first slide of your project presentation. The title slide introduces your project to the audience and provides important information about your research.

The first slide should be simple, attractive and contain only the necessary information. Avoid adding too much text on the title slide.

Information Required on the Title Slide

  • Project title.
  • School name.
  • Subject name.
  • Group members' names.
  • Year of the project.

Example Title Slide

POOR WASTE MANAGEMENT
IN OUR SCHOOL

Grade 10 ICT Project

Prepared by:

1. Group Member One
2. Group Member Two
3. Group Member Three
4. Group Member Four
5. Group Member Five

Naikuru P.A.G Secondary School

2026

Steps for Creating a Title Slide in Microsoft PowerPoint

  1. Open Microsoft PowerPoint.
  2. Select Blank Presentation.
  3. Choose a suitable slide layout such as Title Slide.
  4. Type your project title in the title section.
  5. Enter group members' names and school details.
  6. Apply suitable font size and formatting.
  7. Insert a relevant image if necessary.
  8. Save your presentation as GroupXX_Task3_Presentation.pptx.

LibreOffice Impress: same steps (File → New → Presentation → Title Slide). Google Slides: slides.google.com → Blank → Title slide.

Good Title Slide Practices

  • Use a clear and readable title.
  • Use large font sizes.
  • Avoid too many colours.
  • Use relevant images only.
  • Keep the slide neat and organised.

Activity

Create the first slide of your ICT Project presentation using Microsoft PowerPoint. Include all required information and apply appropriate formatting. Replace the example names with your real group members.
