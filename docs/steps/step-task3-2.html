TASK 3: PROJECT PRESENTATION

STEP 2: PROBLEM DESCRIPTION SLIDE (SLIDE 2)

Slide 2 explains the problem you investigated. Use short bullets and one photo — you will speak the detail during the oral presentation.

Example: Waste Management (adapt to your topic)

Problem Description

Poor Waste Management in Our School

  • Plastic and papers behind classes and canteen
  • Food waste attracts flies; piles when collection delays
  • Cleanliness rating only 2.75 / 5; just 40% use bins

[Photo corner: litter spot, faces blurred]

Naikuru P.A.G • Grade 10 ICT
2 / 6

Steps

  1. Insert → New Slide → Title and Content.
  2. Type 3–4 bullets (no paragraphs). Keep each under 10 words.
  3. Insert → Pictures → your compound photo → resize to corner.
  4. Font 28pt+ title, 24pt+ body. High contrast.
  5. Animations → select bullets → Appear (one by one).

Topic swaps

  • Road safety: 55% walk, 30% boda; no zebra at gate.
  • Time: study 1.8h vs phone 2.6h; 50% have no timetable.
  • Health: handwash Always 35%; toilet rating 2.6/5.
  • Business: no daily records; weekly profit unknown.

Activity

Build Slide 2 for YOUR problem with 3 bullets + 1 photo. View examples: Waste • Road • Time.
