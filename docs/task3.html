TASK 3 OVERVIEW: PROJECT PRESENTATION

Task 3 involves preparing and presenting your project findings using presentation software such as Microsoft PowerPoint, Google Slides or LibreOffice Impress.

In this task, learners will transform the information collected, analysed and written in the previous tasks into a clear and attractive presentation. The presentation should communicate the problem investigated, research findings, proposed solutions and conclusion.

The presentation should be well organised, visually appealing and should allow the audience to understand the project easily.

What is Required in Task 3?

Learners are expected to create a presentation containing between 5 to 8 slides covering the following sections:

  • Create a title slide containing project information.
  • Explain the identified problem.
  • Present research findings using tables and charts.
  • Explain causes and effects of the problem.
  • Present proposed solutions.
  • Give a conclusion and recommendations.
  • Apply slide designs, transitions and animations.
  • Present the project to the audience.

Recommended Slide Arrangement

SlideContent
Slide 1Title of the project, school name, group members and year.
Slide 2Description of the problem being investigated.
Slide 3Research findings using charts, graphs or tables.
Slide 4Causes and effects of the identified problem.
Slide 5Proposed solutions to address the problem.
Slide 6Conclusion and recommendations.

1. Waste Management: litter, bins, sorting and composting in the school compound.

2. Environmental Conservation: tree planting, soil erosion and greening.

3. Health & Safety: handwashing, clean water, toilets and first aid.

4. Time Management: study hours, phone distraction and timetables.

5. Road Safety: crossing, boda-boda helmets and the school gate road.

6. Small Business Records: canteen sales, stock and profit.

Worked example — Topic: Poor Waste Management in School

Problem:
Poor waste disposal practices have affected cleanliness and environmental conditions within the school.

Presentation Should Show:

  • The causes of poor waste management.
  • The effects of poor waste disposal.
  • Data collected from learners and teachers.
  • Charts showing research findings.
  • Solutions for improving waste management.

View it exactly as PowerPoint: Waste example deck (6 slides) • Road: Road deck • Time: Time deck

Presentation Requirements

  • Use appropriate slide layouts.
  • Use readable font sizes (24pt+ body).
  • Include relevant images.
  • Avoid too much text on slides.
  • Use transitions between slides.
  • Apply animations appropriately.
  • Ensure all group members participate during presentation.

Expected Final Output

At the end of Task 3, learners should have:

  • A completed PowerPoint presentation.
  • Slides containing project information.
  • Charts and images supporting findings.
  • A presentation ready for delivery.
