# Oral Presentation Tips (Task 3, part 4)

- 6–8 minutes total; each of the 5 members speaks (~1 min each).
- Roles: introducer, problem + findings, chart explainer, solutions, conclusion + Q&A.
- Face the audience, greet, state group/problem, then show chart and explain ONE takeaway.
- Do not read slides word-for-word. Use short notes/cards.
- Rehearse transitions/animations; test projector/HDMI before assembly.
- Teacher confirms participation for marks.
