# Mail Merge Letter Template (sample)
## In Word/LibreOffice: Mailings > Start Mail Merge > Letters > Select Recipients (use `mail-merge-recipients.csv`)

---

Dear «FirstName» «LastName» («Class»),

**RE: Our Grade 10 ICT Project Report — Waste Management at Naikuru P.A.G Secondary**

Thank you for supporting community service learning. Our group investigated waste
disposal in the school compound (20 participants, average cleanliness 2.75/5).

Please find attached our full report (`GroupXX_Task2_Report.docx`) and a one-page summary
of proposed solutions: more labelled bins, duty rota, environment club, compost pit,
and cleanest-class recognition.

We welcome your feedback before our oral presentation. Kindly reply to our patron
or class teacher by Friday.

Yours faithfully,

**Group XX — Grade 10 ICT**
Naikuru P.A.G Secondary School
Date: October 2026

---

### Steps to finish mail merge (Word)
1. Mailings > Select Recipients > Use an Existing List > choose `mail-merge-recipients.csv`.
2. Insert Merge Fields: FirstName, LastName, Class.
3. Finish & Merge > Edit Individual Documents > All → saves all personalised letters.
4. Save that output as `GroupXX_Task2_MailMerge.docx` (at least 10 letters).

### LibreOffice Writer
Tools > Mail Merge Wizard (or Ctrl+Shift+M in newer versions) > select CSV > insert fields > save merged document.
