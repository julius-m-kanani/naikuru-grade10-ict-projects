### Quick Start for Learners

1. Form a group of **up to 5 members** (teacher to approve).
2. Pick **ONE** community problem. Recommended for Naikuru:
   - Waste management in school compound
   - Environmental conservation (tree planting / soil erosion)
   - Health and safety awareness
   - Time management among learners
   - Road safety (Naikuru centre road)
   - Small business record keeping (kiosks / canteen)
3. Collect data from **at least 20 participants** (Task 1).
4. Analyse in a **spreadsheet**, write a **word-processed report**, build a **5–8 slide presentation**, then pack everything in **one e-portfolio folder** (Tasks 1–4).
5. Submit a soft copy to your teacher as directed. Check accuracy before submitting.
